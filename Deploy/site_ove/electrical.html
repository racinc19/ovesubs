Installed Electrical Panel Elevation

Install basis: four designed Square D / Schneider NQ breaker panelboards plus a side control-panel bank. The 24V tape supplies are split at two Mean Well SDR-480-24 supplies per 20A 120V breaker, so the 12 supplies require six 20A lighting/control breakers before they feed the fused 24V outputs and Shelly modules. This schedule defines the equipment and cabinet build; the installer is to install this basis of design, label it, and submit any required code substitutions before rough-in.
Sizing rule: breaker-space count is based on the current circuit schedules plus a spare allowance; amp rating is based on calculated load. Do not add the four panel main ratings together as the actual building load. Current schedule basis remains Panel 1 at 300A, Panel 2 at 300A, Panel 3 at 225A, and Panel 4 at 225A, fed from the main service/transformer load calculation. Panels 3 and 4 are shown as 30-space because that is the tighter purchasable 225A Square D NQ single-phase/copper basis; going below 30 spaces requires a verified factory/custom panelboard package or a different listed panel family.
Mounting clarification: the four house breaker panels are normal breaker panelboards with breakers mounted on the panelboard bus, not DIN rail. The side control cabinets are DIN rail cabinets: Mean Well SDR supplies, Phoenix fuse terminals, Shelly Pro RGBWW PM controllers, distribution terminals, and output terminals mount on NS35 DIN rails with wire duct and back panels.
